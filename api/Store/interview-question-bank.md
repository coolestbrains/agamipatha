# Interview question bank

Practise out loud. Keep answers under two minutes.

## Common HR

- Walk me through your education so far.
- Why this field, and why now?
- Tell me about a project that did not go as planned.
- Where do you want to be three years from now?

## Engineering

- Explain a project as if I am not a developer.
- How would you debug a slow API?
- What is the difference between an array and a hash map?

## Commerce and CA

- Walk through a simple profit-and-loss statement.
- What does GST mean for a small shop?
- How do you stay current with ICAI notifications?

## Civil services and law

- Summarise one current national issue in 90 seconds.
- How does a bill become an act?
- What does Article 21 protect?

About AgamiPatha

AgamiPatha (Agami Patha) is a self-serve career guide for students and professionals — map Indian education and career routes from where you stand to the profession you want.

What this is

A practical, self-serve career guide. It shows qualifications, exams, years, and typical cost on real Indian routes after Class 10, Class 12, graduation, or a working standing — not a generic quiz and not a booked counselling session.

Who it helps

Students choosing a stream or first career, professionals planning a next role or switch, and parents guiding alongside. Start from the standing you already hold and map the hops yourself.

Who builds it

AgamiPatha is built by Coolest Brains. The catalogue is curated and kept editable so the map stays useful as rules and programmes change.

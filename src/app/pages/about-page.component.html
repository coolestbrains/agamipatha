About

AgamiPatha maps Indian education routes - from where a student stands to the profession they want to reach.

What this is

A practical path planner for students, parents, and mentors. It shows qualifications, exams, years, and typical cost on real Indian routes - not a generic career quiz.

Who builds it

AgamiPatha is built by Coolest Brains. The catalogue is curated and kept editable so the map stays useful as rules and programmes change.

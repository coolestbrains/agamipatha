A self-serve career guide for students and professionals

AgamiPatha (Agami Patha) helps you map an Indian career path yourself — from Class 10, Class 12, a degree, or a working standing to a profession — with exams, years, and typical cost. No counsellor appointment required.

Guide yourself from where you stand

Whether you are choosing a stream after 10th, a course after 12th, a first job after graduation, or a mid-career switch, start from the qualification you already hold. AgamiPatha opens only the doors that come next and draws a stepwise route to the goal you name — a practical self-serve alternative to waiting for counselling slots.
